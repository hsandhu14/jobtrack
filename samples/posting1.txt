Seattle, WA · Hybrid (3 days in office)

Platform team owns the APIs that route millions of deliveries each week. We're looking for an

What we're looking for
- BS in Computer Science or equivalent experience (new grads welcome)
- Proficiency in Python
- Experience with SQL databases, ideally PostgreSQL
- Familiarity with Git and CI/CD pipelines
- Nice to have: Kubernetes, Redis, TypeScript

Compensation: $115,000 – $140,000 base salary, plus equity and benefits.
